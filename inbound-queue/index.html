Seven items, in the order I'd work them Internal

Seven items arrived in the first weeks on the account. The ordering below is by business impact and urgency, with a tiebreaker on how fast each can be cleared. Routing an item elsewhere doesn't end the TAM's involvement: the TAM still owns the acknowledgment, the handoff, and the follow-through the customer experiences.

Order Item How I'd handle it Why Owner
1 P2 escalation: checkout latency up sharply since yesterday Acknowledge immediately. Engage support and engineering, check for platform-side changes, and own customer communication on a set cadence until resolved. Checkout is revenue and it is degrading right now. TAM coordinates; support and engineering investigate
2 Support ticket about a failed deploy, sent directly to me Warm handoff to support with a ticket opened on the customer's behalf. Flag the possible link to the latency escalation. Same day as the P2, and "since yesterday" plus a failed deploy may be one incident. Support; TAM stays copied
3 Cost anomaly in the usage dashboard that nobody has flagged Investigate the dashboard, form a hypothesis, and bring findings to the customer before they see it on an invoice. Catching cost movement early is the TAM's core value. The AE only gets involved if it turns commercial. TAM
4 "When is feature X shipping? We need a date for our board deck" Get the official position from Product, return with what can be shared (roadmap stage, not a committed date), and offer a PM conversation if they need more. Date-bound and quick to unblock. An unofficial date must never reach a board. TAM with Product
5 "Can you join our Tuesday and Thursday standups and pair with us on the migration?" Decline the standing cadence. Offer a migration kickoff, an architecture review, and office hours at milestones. If they need embedded engineers, route to professional services through the AE. Set expectations early so the team plans around real support. TAM; AE for services
6 Request to run a training session for a newly hired frontend team Scope it with the customer (caching and function configuration belong on the agenda), route delivery to the enablement team, and open or co-present. Valuable but not urgent. Scoping is what makes it land. Enablement; TAM scopes
7 Question from a different business unit about a product outside my workload Answer if trivial. Otherwise introduce them to the account team. An expansion signal for the AE, not part of this workload. AE

Timing: items 1 and 2 are the same day. Items 3 and 4 are the same week. Items 5 through 7 get scheduled.
